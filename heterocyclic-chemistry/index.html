What it is

The field in one minute

Heterocyclic chemistry studies ring molecules that contain at least one atom other than carbon, most often nitrogen, oxygen or sulfur.

These rings are everywhere in life and medicine: the bases of DNA, haem, chlorophyll, vitamins and most small-molecule drugs. Replacing one ring carbon with a nitrogen changes basicity, shape and the way a molecule binds its target.

59%of FDA-approved small-molecule drugs contain a nitrogen heterocycle (2014 analysis)
