AI in chemistry, in one minute

This page tracks AI in chemistry where it has actually delivered: real molecules, real materials and real decisions, not just promises.

Each use case is judged by evidence: was the prediction tested in the lab, did it beat existing methods, and did it change what chemists do? From antibiotic discovery to protein design and materials screening, the record is growing fast, along with lessons from failures.
