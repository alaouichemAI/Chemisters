What it is

The field in one minute

Entropy measures how many ways the energy and particles of a system can be arranged, and it decides the direction of every spontaneous change.

Ice melts in a warm room, gases mix and perfume spreads because those outcomes can happen in vastly more ways than the alternatives. Combined with enthalpy in the Gibbs energy, entropy predicts which reactions run on their own and where equilibrium settles.
