What it is

The field in one minute

Bioorganic chemistry uses the tools of organic chemistry to understand how living systems make and use molecules.

It studies how enzymes catalyse reactions, how cells build natural products and how small molecules can probe or switch biological processes. It connects the flask to the cell.

1894Emil Fischer proposes the lock-and-key model of enzymes
1953the Miller–Urey experiment makes amino acids from simple gases
2022Nobel Prize for click and bioorthogonal chemistry
