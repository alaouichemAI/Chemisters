What it is

The field in one minute

Environmental chemistry studies chemicals in air, water and soil: where they come from, how they move and change, and what they do to living things.

It explains the ozone hole, acid rain, smog, climate forcing and the fate of pesticides, plastics and 'forever chemicals'. Its measurements underpin environmental law and the cleanup of polluted sites.
