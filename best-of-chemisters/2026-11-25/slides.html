IR relaxation times assigned to contact motions

University of Freiburg · University of Zurich
IR ↔ contact motions
MD networks match IR
Kinetics linked to motions
For Protein IR spectroscopists
What they did

We combined transient infrared spectroscopy with nonequilibrium molecular dynamics simulations to connect experimental relaxation times to local inter‑residue contact distances.

Why it matters

Contact‑distance networks capture coordinated motions that mediate long‑range allosteric coupling, allowing each kinetic step to be assigned to a specific molecular motion.

IR ↔ contact motionsMD networks match IRKinetics linked to motions
Term to knowInter‑residue contact distance: Distance between specific atom pairs of two residues used to monitor structural changes in proteins.

Quinazoline hydrogenation: 98% yield, 99% ee

Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Yangzhou University +2
Up to 98% yield
Up to 99% ee
10 ppm catalyst loading
For Catalysis and medicinal chemists
What they did

A solo‑coordination‑vacant Ir(III) pincer catalyst enables catalytic asymmetric partial hydrogenation of quinazolines, delivering enantioenriched 3,4‑dihydroquinazolines.

Why it matters

Product‑induced catalyst deactivation has limited quinazoline reductions; this robust Ir(III) pincer catalyst overcomes poisoning, allowing broad substrate scope, high yields, and ultra‑low loadings (10 ppm, TON 38 000).

Hollow COF with H2PO3 achieves 0.5 ng L⁻¹ OCP detection

Fuzhou University · Wuyi University +1
One-step hollow COF
α‑aminophosphonic links
Detect OCPs 0.1‑0.5 ng L⁻¹
For Analytical chemists and environmental…
What they did

The study converts imine bonds into α‑aminophosphonic acid linkages via linkage hydrophosphonylation, forming hollow COF spheres with H2PO3 groups in a single step.

Why it matters

The hollow structure increases surface area and shortens mass‑transfer pathways, while H2PO3 sites provide multiple non‑covalent interactions (van der Waals, π‑π, hydrogen‑bond, dipole, halogen), yielding rapid, high‑affinity OCP capture and sub‑ng L⁻¹ detection.

Mem‑OV gives 734 nm & 514 nm for ONOO− and viscosity

Central China Normal University
Dual-channel fluorescence
734 nm viscosity signal
514 nm ONOO− signal
For Analytical chemists and bioimaging…
What they did

The probe Mem‑OV rapidly turns on fluorescence at 734 nm for viscosity and at 514 nm for ONOO−, with no channel crosstalk and strong membrane localization.

Why it matters

Because Mem‑OV simultaneously visualizes viscosity and ONOO− at the membrane, it can track ferroptosis dynamics, differentiate tumor from normal cells, and be applied in vivo through injection.
