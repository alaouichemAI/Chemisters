High‑EState electrolytes cut SEI loss to 6% & 99.98% CE

University of Cambridge · Nanjing University · China · UK
SEI loss ↓ to 6%
99.98% CE over 1000 cycles
EState predicts SEI loss
For Battery and electrolyte researchers
What they did

We measured SEI dissolution for many Na‑ion electrolytes, used a closed‑loop data‑driven workflow to link dissolution to >200 physicochemical descriptors, and identified the solvent average EState index as the key predictor.

Why it matters

SEI dissolution depletes Na inventory, causing rapid capacity loss; minimizing dissolution with high‑EState electrolytes reduces Na loss and enables near‑unity Coulombic efficiency, extending battery life.

SEI loss ↓ to 6%99.98% CE over 1000 cyclesEState predicts SEI loss

City University of Hong Kong · The Chinese University of Hong Kong
C–Si bond cleavage

For Synthetic chemists and redox specialists
What they did

Synthesized a bowl‑shaped B–N tetraquinane in one high‑yield step and measured a reduction potential of −3.2 V vs Fc/Fc+, a record for organic reductants.

Why it matters

Topological strain in the 3‑D bowl creates a highly destabilized boron dianion, giving exceptionally negative reduction potential and enabling reductive activation of strong C–Si, C–C, and C–F bonds.
