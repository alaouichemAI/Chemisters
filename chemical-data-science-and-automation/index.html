What it is

The field in one minute

Chemical data science turns the data chemistry produces, from instruments, experiments and the literature, into reliable knowledge, and automates the pipelines that handle it.

Clean, well-described data is the fuel of every model and every automated lab. This field covers data capture, standards, electronic lab notebooks, analysis pipelines and statistics, so that results are findable and reproducible.
