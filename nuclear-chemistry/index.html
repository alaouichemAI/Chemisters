What it is

The field in one minute

Nuclear chemistry studies radioactivity, nuclear reactions and the chemistry of radioactive elements.

It made possible nuclear power, radiocarbon dating, and medical imaging and therapy with radioisotopes. It also deals with the heaviest elements, nuclear waste and radioactivity in the environment.

5,730 yearsthe half-life of carbon-14
