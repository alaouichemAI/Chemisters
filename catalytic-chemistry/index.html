What it is

The field in one minute

Catalysis speeds up chemical reactions without the catalyst being used up, and it can steer them toward one product.

Most industrial chemical processes rely on at least one catalyst, from fertilizer and fuels to plastics and medicines. Enzymes, metal complexes, solid surfaces and small organic molecules can all play the role.

1835Berzelius coins the word 'catalysis'
1909Fritz Haber demonstrates catalytic ammonia synthesis
2021Nobel Prize for asymmetric organocatalysis
