What it is

The field in one minute

Analytical chemistry answers two questions about any sample: what is in it, and how much.

It provides the methods every other field depends on, from the glucose meter and the doping test to the spectrum that confirms a new drug's structure. A good method is judged by three words: accurate, precise and sensitive.

Branches

The map of Analytical Chemistry

Each branch is a world of its own. Several have their own Chemisters page — follow the ones you care about.

Chromatography

Separating mixtures by how strongly each component sticks to a stationary phase.

HPLC checks the purity of almost every medicine.

Mass spectrometry

Weighing molecules as ions to identify them.

Doping tests and protein analysis rely on it.

UV–visible & IR spectroscopy

Identifying and measuring substances through the light they absorb.

The Beer–Lambert law turns absorbance into concentration.

NMR spectroscopy

Nuclear spins in a magnetic field reveal how atoms are connected.

The standard way to confirm a newly made molecule.
