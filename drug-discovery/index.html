What it is

The field in one minute

Drug discovery is the long road from a disease target to a medicine: finding molecules that act on the target, then making them safe and effective enough for patients.

It usually takes more than a decade and passes through target identification, hit finding, lead optimization, preclinical testing and clinical trials. Most candidates fail along the way, which is why better prediction, from AI to new screening methods, matters so much.
