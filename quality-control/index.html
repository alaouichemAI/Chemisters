What it is

The field in one minute

Quality control checks that every batch of a product meets its specifications, so that what reaches the customer, or the patient, is exactly what the label says.

In chemical and pharmaceutical plants it combines validated analytical methods, sampling plans, statistics and careful documentation. Its rules, Good Manufacturing Practice, exist because past failures harmed people.

1924Walter Shewhart invents the control chart
1938US Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, passed after the elixir sulfanilamide deaths
