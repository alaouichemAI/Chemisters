What it is

The field in one minute

Industrial chemistry turns chemical reactions into products at scale, safely and economically: fertilizers, fuels, plastics, metals and medicines.

It is where laboratory discoveries meet reactors, supply chains, energy costs and regulation. A handful of large processes, making ammonia, sulfuric acid, ethylene and chlorine, underpin much of the material economy.

1746John Roebuck's lead-chamber process starts industrial sulfuric acid
1913the first Haber–Bosch ammonia plant opens at Oppau, Germany
About halfof the world's people are fed thanks to synthetic nitrogen fertilizer, by Vaclav Smil's estimate
