What it is

The field in one minute

Forensic chemistry applies analytical chemistry to legal questions: what is this substance, where did it come from, and will the result stand up in court?

It identifies drugs, poisons, fibres, paints and inks, and links traces to their sources. Every result must be reproducible, documented and defensible under cross-examination.
