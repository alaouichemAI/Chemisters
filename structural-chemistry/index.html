What it is

The field in one minute

Structural chemistry determines how atoms are arranged in space, in molecules and in crystals, and links that arrangement to properties.

Shape decides almost everything: how a drug fits its target, why diamond is hard and graphite slippery, how DNA stores information. X-ray crystallography, NMR and electron microscopy are its eyes.

1912Max von Laue shows that crystals diffract X-rays
1953the DNA double helix is built from X-ray diffraction data
200,000+structures stored in the Protein Data Bank
