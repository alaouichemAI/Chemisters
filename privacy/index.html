Who We Are

Chemisters is a network of chemistry pages on social media, together with the website chemisters.com. It is operated by Mohamed El Belghiti Alaoui, Rabat, Morocco, who is responsible for the processing described in this policy.

For anything in this policy, contact us at contact@chemisters.com

In Short

  • chemisters.com sets no cookies of its own, shows no ads, and has no accounts or newsletter.
  • We only receive personal data that you send us yourself, by email or by message to one of our pages.
  • We never sell or rent personal data, and never use it for advertising.
  • Our TikTok integration is used only to publish videos to Chemisters' own TikTok accounts.

This Website

chemisters.com is a static website: it has no accounts, no forms that store data, and no analytics tools.

As with any website, the companies that host and deliver it receive technical data when you visit: your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. They use it to deliver the pages and protect the site against abuse. See Service Providers.

If we add visitor statistics in the future, we will describe them on this page before turning them on.

When You Contact Us

When you write to us by email or through one of our pages, we receive your name, your email address or profile, and the content of your message. We use it only to reply and to follow up on your request.

Research Submissions

When you ask us to feature your research, you send us your post text, an image, your name and profile link, your co-authors' names and profile links, and a link to your own post about the article. We use this to check your submission against our requirements and, once it meets them, to publish it.

Once published, the post, the names, and the tags are public on the Chemisters page where it appears. By sending your co-authors' names, you confirm that they agree to be named and tagged.

You can ask us at any time to remove a published post. We remove it from our page as soon as possible.

Our Social Media Pages

Chemisters has pages on several social media platforms. When you follow, like, comment on, or message one of them, that platform processes your data under its own privacy policy.

As page administrators, we can see your public profile, your public interactions, and the messages you send us, plus anonymous, aggregated audience statistics provided by the platform. We do not export or collect follower data from these platforms.

TikTok Integration

Chemisters has a TikTok application that publishes videos produced by Chemisters to TikTok accounts owned and operated by Chemisters. It is not offered to other TikTok users.

When a Chemisters account authorizes the application, TikTok gives us access to:

PermissionWhat it gives usWhy
user.info.basicThe account's open ID, display name, and avatarTo confirm which Chemisters account is connected
video.uploadPermission to upload video files to the accountTo send our videos to TikTok
video.publishPermission to publish videos on the accountTo post our videos with their captions

Access tokens are stored securely on our own server, used only for publishing, and never shared. We do not access other users' data, messages, or viewing activity. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app's settings; the tokens then stop working and we delete them.

Service Providers

These companies process data on our behalf, or when you use their services alongside ours. Some are based outside Morocco, mainly in the United States.

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts this website.
  • Cloudflare: delivers and protects this website.
  • The social media platforms where Chemisters has pages, such as LinkedIn and Facebook (Meta): host our pages and messages.
  • TikTok: hosts the videos published through our integration.
  • Our email provider: handles messages sent to contact@chemisters.com.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Emails and messages: as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than two years after our last exchange.
  • Research submissions: while the post is published on our pages. Submissions that are never published are deleted within six months.
  • TikTok tokens: while the authorization is active; deleted when it is revoked or expires.

Your Privacy Rights

Under Moroccan Law No. 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing for legitimate reasons. If you live in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you similar rights, including data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@chemisters.com. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Moroccan data protection authority, the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel), or to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

Children's Privacy

Chemisters is meant for students, researchers, and professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will also announce it on our main pages.

Questions or concerns? If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at contact@chemisters.com.
