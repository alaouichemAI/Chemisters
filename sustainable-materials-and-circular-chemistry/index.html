What it is

The field in one minute

Circular chemistry designs materials and processes so that resources stay in use: made from renewable or recycled feedstocks, and recovered at the end of their life instead of becoming waste.

Today most plastics are used once and thrown away. Circular chemistry develops recyclable-by-design polymers, chemical recycling, bio-based feedstocks and ways to recover metals from electronics and batteries.

About 9%of all plastic ever made had been recycled, by a 2017 estimate
2016a bacterium that digests PET plastic is reported
2019twelve principles of circular chemistry are proposed
