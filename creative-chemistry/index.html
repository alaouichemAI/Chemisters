What this page is

Creative chemistry, in one minute

Creative Chemistry explores where chemistry meets art, design and unconventional thinking: pigments, fireworks, perfume, photography, and the creativity behind discovery itself.

Every colour on a painter's palette, every firework and every fragrance is applied chemistry. And designing a new molecule is itself a creative act, closer to architecture than to following a recipe.
