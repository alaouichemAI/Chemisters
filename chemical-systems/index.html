What it is

The field in one minute

Chemical Systems explores reactions that do not simply go from A to B: networks with feedback, oscillating clocks, travelling waves, patterns and chaos.

Far from equilibrium, chemistry can keep time, form stripes and spots, and even compute. The same principles help explain heartbeats, circadian rhythms and the patterns on animal coats.

1951Boris Belousov observes an oscillating reaction
1952Alan Turing publishes 'The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis'
1977Nobel Prize to Ilya Prigogine for dissipative structures
