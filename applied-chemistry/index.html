What it is

The field in one minute

Applied chemistry puts chemical knowledge to work on practical problems: cleaner water, better crops, safer products and longer-lasting materials.

It spans agriculture, cosmetics, paints, detergents, textiles, construction and countless everyday products. Where basic research asks 'why?', applied chemistry asks 'how do we make this work, at a price people can pay?'
