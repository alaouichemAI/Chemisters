What this page is

Serendipity, in one minute

Some of chemistry's biggest breakthroughs came from failed experiments, contaminated samples and things nobody was looking for.

A purple dye from a failed attempt at quinine, Teflon from a gas cylinder that seemed empty, penicillin from a contaminated plate. The common thread is not luck alone, but someone who noticed something odd and asked why.
