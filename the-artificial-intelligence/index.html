What it is

AI in one minute

Artificial intelligence is the science of building systems that learn from data, recognize patterns, make decisions and generate content.

This page follows the AI advances that matter for chemistry and science: new models, tools and methods, explained for scientists. Breakthroughs from deep learning to large language models often reach chemistry labs within months.
