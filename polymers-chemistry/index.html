What it is

The field in one minute

Polymer chemistry studies giant molecules made of repeating units: plastics, rubbers, fibres, gels and the biopolymers of life.

Linking thousands of small units creates materials with properties no small molecule has: stretch, strength, toughness and flexibility. Polymer chemists control chain length, architecture and composition, and increasingly design polymers that can be recycled or degrade safely.

1920Hermann Staudinger proposes that polymers are long covalent chains
