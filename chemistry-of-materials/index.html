What it is

The field in one minute

Materials chemistry designs and makes substances with useful properties: strong, light, conductive, magnetic, transparent, porous or responsive.

It connects atomic structure to performance, from battery electrodes and semiconductors to ceramics, glasses, alloys and porous frameworks. New materials often decide which technologies become possible.
