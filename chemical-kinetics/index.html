What it is

The field in one minute

Chemical kinetics measures how fast reactions go and uncovers the step-by-step path they follow.

Thermodynamics says whether a reaction can happen; kinetics says whether it happens in a second or in a million years. Rate laws, activation energies and mechanisms let chemists speed up the reactions they want and slow down the ones they don't, from drug degradation to engine combustion.
