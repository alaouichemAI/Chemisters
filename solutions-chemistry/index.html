What it is

The field in one minute

Solutions chemistry studies what happens when one substance dissolves in another: solubility, concentration and the new properties the mixture gains.

Most chemistry, and all of biology, happens in solution. Why salt dissolves in water but not in oil, why seawater freezes below 0 °C and how cells keep their shape all come down to the interactions between solvent and solute.
