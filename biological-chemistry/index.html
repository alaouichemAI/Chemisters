What it is

The field in one minute

Biological chemistry, or biochemistry, explains life in molecular terms: how proteins, nucleic acids, lipids and sugars are built and how they react inside cells.

Enzymes speed up reactions by factors of millions, DNA stores information, membranes control what enters a cell and metabolism turns food into usable energy. These molecules underlie modern medicine, biotechnology and drug discovery.
