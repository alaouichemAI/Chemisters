What it is

The field in one minute

Green chemistry designs products and processes that reduce or eliminate hazardous substances from the start, instead of cleaning up afterwards.

Its 12 principles cover waste prevention, atom economy, safer solvents, energy efficiency, renewable feedstocks and catalysis. The core idea is simple: the best waste is the waste you never make.
