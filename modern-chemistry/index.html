What it is

The field in one minute

Modern Chemistry follows the advances of recent decades that are changing how chemists think and work: new reactions, new tools, new materials and new ways of discovering.

Click chemistry, organocatalysis, photoredox catalysis, cryo-EM, AI structure prediction, metal–organic frameworks and self-driving labs were not in textbooks a generation ago. This page tracks the ideas reshaping chemistry right now.
