What it is

The field in one minute

Physical chemistry uses the laws of physics to explain why chemical change happens, how fast it goes and how much energy it involves.

It answers three questions about any reaction: can it happen (thermodynamics), how fast will it go (kinetics), and why do molecules behave as they do (quantum mechanics). Batteries, spectra, industrial reaction rates and the colours of molecules are all understood through it.
