What it is

The field in one minute

Chemoinformatics applies information science to chemistry: representing molecules as data, storing and searching them, and predicting their properties.

It powers chemical databases, similarity searches, QSAR and QSPR models and virtual screening. Every time a model predicts solubility or toxicity from a structure, chemoinformatics is at work.
