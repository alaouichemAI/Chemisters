What it is

The field in one minute

Supramolecular chemistry is chemistry beyond the molecule: it studies how molecules recognize, bind and assemble through non-covalent interactions.

Hydrogen bonds, π-stacking, metal coordination and the hydrophobic effect let molecules form host–guest complexes, self-assembled cages and even molecular machines. Nature uses the same tools to fold proteins and pair DNA strands.
