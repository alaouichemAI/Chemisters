What it is

The field in one minute

Inorganic chemistry covers the chemistry of all the elements, with a special focus on metals, minerals and compounds not built around carbon chains.

It explains catalysts, pigments, semiconductors, batteries, fertilizers and the metal ions that keep living cells running. From the periodic table to coordination complexes and solid-state materials, it is chemistry's widest map.
