What it is

The field in one minute

Nanochemistry makes and studies materials between about 1 and 100 nanometres in size, where size itself changes colour, reactivity and strength.

A gold nanoparticle can look red, a semiconductor crystal can glow a colour set by its size, and a sheet of carbon one atom thick is remarkably strong. Nanochemists build these structures and put them to work in medicine, electronics, catalysis and energy.
