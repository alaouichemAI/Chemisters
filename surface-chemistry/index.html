What it is

The field in one minute

Surface chemistry studies what happens at the boundary between two phases: solid and gas, solid and liquid, liquid and gas.

Catalysis, corrosion, adhesion, lubrication, cleaning, sensors and chip manufacturing all happen at surfaces. A few atomic layers often decide how a whole material behaves.
