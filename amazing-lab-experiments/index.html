What this page is

Why experiments matter

Amazing Lab Experiments celebrates the experiments that make chemistry visible: colour changes, glowing reactions, oscillating clocks and classic demonstrations, with the chemistry behind each one.

Behind every spectacular demonstration is a principle worth understanding: redox, catalysis, equilibrium, chemiluminescence. Many of these experiments are hazardous and must only be performed by trained people with proper safety equipment.
