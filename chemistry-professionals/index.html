What this page is

Chemistry careers, in one minute

Chemistry Professionals is about careers in chemistry: the roles chemists hold, the skills that matter and how to grow from student to expert.

Chemists work in research labs, plants, regulatory agencies, start-ups, patent offices, data teams and classrooms. Knowing the paths, the skills employers look for and how the field is changing helps chemists make better career decisions.
