What it is

The field in one minute

Organic chemistry is the chemistry of carbon compounds: how they are built, how they react, and how chemists make new ones.

Carbon forms four strong bonds and bonds strongly to itself, so it can grow into chains, rings and 3D frameworks of almost any shape. That one property is why the same field covers fuels, plastics, dyes, DNA and nearly every medicine on a pharmacy shelf. The name is a fossil: it once meant 'compounds only living things can make', until chemists proved otherwise in 1828.
