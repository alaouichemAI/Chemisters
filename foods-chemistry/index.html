What it is

The field in one minute

Food chemistry studies what food is made of and the chemical changes it goes through during cooking, processing and storage.

Why bread crust browns, why cut apples turn brown, how mayonnaise stays together and how preservatives keep food safe: it all comes down to chemistry. The field also measures nutrients, contaminants and allergens.
