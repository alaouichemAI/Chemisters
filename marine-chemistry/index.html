What it is

The field in one minute

Marine chemistry studies the composition of seawater and the chemical processes of the oceans, from dissolved salts and gases to the molecules made by marine life.

The ocean absorbs a large share of the CO₂ we emit, which is making it more acidic. Its organisms also produce unusual molecules, some of which have become medicines.
