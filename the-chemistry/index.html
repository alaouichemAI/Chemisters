What it is

Chemistry in one minute

Chemistry is the science of matter: what substances are made of, how their atoms are bonded, and how they change into other substances.

Everything you can touch is chemistry, from the air you breathe to the screen you are reading. This page explores the foundations every chemist builds on: atoms, bonds, reactions, energy and the periodic table.
