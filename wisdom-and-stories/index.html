What this page is

Why stories matter in science

Wisdom & Stories shares the human side of chemistry: the doubt, persistence, luck and insight behind discoveries, told through chemists past and present.

Papers report results; stories explain how people got there. Seeing how others handled failure, rivalry, mentorship and ethical choices helps every scientist do better work, and stay motivated while doing it.
