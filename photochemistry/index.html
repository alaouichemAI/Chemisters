What it is

The field in one minute

Photochemistry studies reactions started by light: a molecule absorbs a photon, reaches an excited state and does chemistry that is impossible in the dark.

Light drives photosynthesis, vision, vitamin D formation and the ozone layer. In the lab, light lets chemists build strained rings, activate stable bonds at room temperature and turn sunlight into fuels and electricity.

400–700 nmthe range of light that plants use for photosynthesis
