What it is

The field in one minute

Chemical engineering designs and runs the processes that turn raw materials into useful products, at scales of tonnes per hour.

It combines chemistry with mass and energy balances, fluid flow, heat transfer, separations, reaction engineering and control. Chemical engineers scale up drugs, fuels, food, water treatment and batteries, and keep plants safe and efficient.

1888MIT launches the first chemical engineering degree programme
1901George E. Davis publishes 'A Handbook of Chemical Engineering'
1915Arthur D. Little introduces the idea of unit operations
