What it is

The field in one minute

Synthetic chemistry is the science of building molecules on purpose, from simple starting materials to complex targets.

Nearly every medicine, pesticide, dye and advanced material started in a synthesis lab. Chemists plan routes, choose reactions, control selectivity and scale up, increasingly with the help of automation and AI.

1828Wöhler makes urea, an organic compound, from an inorganic salt
1972the total synthesis of vitamin B12 is completed by Woodward and Eschenmoser
1990Nobel Prize to E. J. Corey for the theory and methodology of organic synthesis
