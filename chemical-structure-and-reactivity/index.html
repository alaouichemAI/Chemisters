What it is

The field in one minute

This field connects a molecule's structure to the way it reacts: where the electrons sit decides where, how fast and how selectively bonds break and form.

Electronic effects, steric bulk and orbital overlap explain why one carbon reacts and its neighbour doesn't. Mastering these patterns lets chemists predict products instead of memorizing them.
