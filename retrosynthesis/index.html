What it is

The field in one minute

Retrosynthesis plans a synthesis backwards: start from the target molecule and cut it into simpler pieces until you reach materials you can buy.

Formalized by E. J. Corey in the 1960s, it turned synthesis planning from intuition into logic. Today computer programs and AI models search huge numbers of possible disconnections and suggest routes in seconds.

⇒the retrosynthetic arrow, meaning 'can be made from'
