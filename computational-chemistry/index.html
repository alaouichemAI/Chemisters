What it is

The field in one minute

Computational chemistry uses computers to calculate the structures, energies and behaviour of molecules and materials.

From quantum calculations on a single molecule to simulations of millions of atoms, it lets chemists test ideas before going to the lab, explain experiments and screen thousands of candidates for drugs, catalysts and materials.

1970Gaussian 70, the first release of the Gaussian program
1998Nobel Prize for density functional theory and computational quantum chemistry
2013Nobel Prize for multiscale models of complex chemical systems
