What it is

The field in one minute

Thermochemistry measures the heat that flows in and out of chemical reactions and changes of state.

Every fuel, every cold pack and every metabolic pathway is an energy balance. With enthalpies of formation and Hess's law, chemists can predict the heat of reactions they have never run.
