What it is

The field in one minute

This page covers how machine learning is applied across chemistry: predicting properties, planning syntheses, generating new molecules and speeding up simulations.

Models learn patterns from data on molecules and reactions, then make predictions in milliseconds that would take hours of computation or weeks of lab work. The hard parts are good data, honest validation and knowing when a model is out of its depth.
