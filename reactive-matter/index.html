What it is

The field in one minute

Reactive Matter covers the short-lived, high-energy species that chemistry runs on: radicals, carbocations, carbanions and carbenes.

Most reactions pass through intermediates that live for microseconds or less. Learning to generate, observe and tame them has given chemists new ways to build bonds, from radical polymerization to photoredox and electrochemical synthesis.
