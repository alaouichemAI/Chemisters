What it is

The field in one minute

Autonomous chemistry combines robotics, automation and AI so that experiments can be planned, run and analysed with little or no human intervention.

In a self-driving lab, an algorithm chooses the next experiment, robots carry it out, instruments measure the result and the algorithm learns from it, around the clock. The goal is to discover molecules and materials many times faster than by hand.
