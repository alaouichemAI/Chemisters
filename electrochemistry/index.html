What it is

The field in one minute

Electrochemistry studies reactions that move electrons through a circuit: reactions that make electricity, and electricity that drives reactions.

Batteries, fuel cells, electrolysers, corrosion, sensors and electroplating all run on redox reactions at electrodes. It sits at the centre of the energy transition, from lithium-ion batteries to green hydrogen.
