About

The digital ecosystem of chemistry

Founded in 2025, Chemisters connects the chemistry community through reliable information, emerging technologies, and researchers telling their own stories.

More than a science blog, it is a network for the researchers, students, and builders of chemistry's future, and a direct way for authors to reach the people working in their field.

It keeps growing: new pages open as new fields take shape, and existing pages gain new kinds of posts.

Mohamed El Belghiti Alaoui

Founder · PhD student, AI for Alzheimer's disease

Chemisters was founded and is run by Mohamed El Belghiti Alaoui, a PhD student working on artificial intelligence for Alzheimer's disease.

Trained as a pharmacochemist, he builds machine-learning tools for drug discovery: AI agents that predict reaction outcomes and optimise synthesis conditions, and a computational pipeline covering property prediction, molecule generation, toxicity and retrosynthesis. He has published open-access work on how scaffold and random data splits change the evaluation of molecular machine-learning models.
