What it is

The field in one minute

Molecular Systems studies collections of molecules that interact to produce behaviour no single molecule shows: self-replication, molecular machines, networks and emergent function.

Life is the ultimate molecular system. Chemists now build simpler ones in the lab, from self-replicating molecules to motors and pumps, to understand how complex behaviour emerges and to create adaptive materials.
