What it is

The field in one minute

Energy chemistry studies how energy is stored, converted and released in chemical form: fuels, batteries, hydrogen and solar materials.

Moving from fossil fuels to low-carbon energy is, above all, a chemistry problem. Better battery electrodes, cheaper hydrogen catalysts, efficient solar absorbers and carbon capture all start in the chemistry lab.
