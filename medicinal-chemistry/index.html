What it is

The field in one minute

Medicinal chemistry designs, makes and optimizes the molecules that become medicines.

It sits between synthesis, biology and pharmacology. Medicinal chemists adjust a molecule's structure until it binds its target, reaches it in the body, lasts long enough and stays safe. Most small-molecule drugs on the market came out of this cycle.
